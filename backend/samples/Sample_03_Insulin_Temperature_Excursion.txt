COLD CHAIN BREACH & BIOLOGIC DEFECT REPORT
Reporter: Fortis Healthcare Endocrinology Ward, Gurgaon
Email: quality@fortishealthcare.com

Product: Insulin Glargine 100 IU/ml Injection
Batch Number: INS88210
Expiry Date: Nov 2027

Description:
Upon receiving shipment of Insulin Glargine 100 IU/ml (Batch INS88210), temperature data logger reading revealed an excursion up to 14.2°C for over 5 hours during air freight transfer. Physical inspection of 3 vials showed visible cloudiness and fine protein flakes suspended in liquid. The ward has frozen usage of the entire batch shipment pending QA risk assessment and health authority guidance.
